# REFERENT-FIRST: the rules that stop the circle, and where each one is enforced
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session at its user's direction ("stop making these mistakes, burn out the sources of the mistakes and wire this in deep"). A rule with no guard is a wish; every rule below names the file or test that enforces it. If a guard is missing, that is a bug in this file.

## The principle
A referent is a BEING: its identity is its birth address, never its spelling (THEORY-IDENTITY section 1). Two mentions are the same thing for an asker when the UNIVERSE folded on them is the same, bounded only by differences that make no difference to that asker (khora identity-induction.js: the universe is widened hop by hop and stops where widening no longer moves the verdict beyond its null; THEORY-IDENTITY definition 12, the cut). Strings, embeddings and word folds are NOMINATORS: they order attention and are never evidence and never judges. Janus structures (Existence and Structure cells only: NUL SIG INS SEG CON SYN) and never convicts; Penelope DEFs notes, judges (EVA) and revises (REC).

## The rules
1. **The unit is the being, not the string.** Every experiment names the referent object it builds (what is the being, and what is in its universe) and the evidence type it uses (claims, bonds, bindings, grounds, or a declared given with a named giver).
2. **The universe has a stopping rule, not a constant.** A profile's reach is set by "the next step changes no verdict at the asker's tolerance", with the tolerance DECLARED and swept. A bare number chosen for convenience (the first 5 sentences, a window of 3) is an ARBITRARY CONSTANT: it may be pre-registered, but it is labelled as such and its result does not speak for the full universe.
3. **Every arm has a strings-only twin.** The same strings with the structure removed (a random profile of the same size, a same-length bag of frequent words, locality alone). An arm is reported only with its twin's number. If it does not beat its twin it was chasing strings.
4. **A benchmark states what it measures.** A gold set labelled by link anchors measures label-to-page knowledge. On DEV, 4 of 58 ZERO-CLEAN gold sentences have an in-document bridge from the label to the referent; the rest need knowledge the document does not hold. Referent understanding is measured where the document holds the evidence: pronoun and description binding, same-versus-other with claims, explicit alias statements. Sentence recall on anchor gold is reported, never mistaken for that.
5. **World knowledge is a given with a giver.** An embedder's prior comes from its training. Its output is a nomination with standing candidate; it never enters the record as evidence and never convicts.
6. **Observing is not defining.** "There is a period here" is Structure (SEG or CON); "this is a sentence" is a DEF and is Penelope's. Janus writes no DEF, EVA or REC; a verdict word anywhere in what Janus leaves is a defect.
7. **No handrolled logic in the definition path.** No regex over text or tags, no word lists, no date parsing: a declared order and a codec are injected. The cube is read through the pharos door; Janus's own code imports no other repo.
8. **Say what is missing only after searching the nexus.** Before "we do not have that", search the sibling repos and the other sessions' worktrees. A claim about another repo's state is checked against that repo, not remembered.
8b. **Read the Fold standalone before designing anything.** The Fold repo (scores-patch-points/the-fold) already holds the answer pipeline (docs/ANSWER-PIPELINE.md: language, frame, read, bind, candidate, FALSIFY by searching for the refutation, reason, realise; the model is not called for slot asks), the grounding-by-meaning study (docs/GROUNDING-BY-MEANING.md), the pre-registered ethos-priors study (docs/ETHOS-PRIORS-STUDY.md), a tested web research module (fold-chat-web.js) and 95 ant experiments under eval/ants. "We have had all this before" is usually true. The inventory (26 docs, 53 eval folders, 95 ant folders, 165 PREREG and RESULTS files) was counted on 2026-11-20 and only 3 docs and 1 file header were read: until the rest is indexed, any claim that the Fold lacks something is unverified.
9. **Stamp before scoring; read a fresh set once.** Label every number DEV, FRESH, HELD-OUT, REUSED or EXPLORATORY. Anything declared after DEV is looked at goes in an addendum stamped before the fresh read. Ask before any download or push, naming package, source and size.
10. **DEF declares, EVA tests, REC revises: do not conflate them.** That two folds make no difference that matters to an asker is, ultimately, a DEF (the owner, 2026-11-20): a declaration that rests on priors and has a named giver, at a categorically higher level than any instance. It is a universal claim over the whole fold, and the grain theorem (khora hl-acquire.js) says a corpus can refute such a claim and never earn it, so it cannot be derived from the evidence: it must be declared by a giver. EVA is the assertion that the evidence STANDS under that declaration at this tolerance (no difference above the tolerance found, by probes that had power; silence is a hole, never agreement); it can only fail to refute, and is always "up to". REC revises the declaration, or applies the identity (a merge), once EVA has refuted or settled. Fold only until the next step stops mattering. A DEF names its asker, its tolerance and its giver.
   WHERE THE CODE STANDS (checked, not assumed): penelope `compare/log.mjs` puts the standard (perspective, ladder) at DEF·Ground, the per-pair verdict "same" at EVA·Figure, the cannot-link at DEF·Figure and the merge at REC·Figure; `THEORY-IDENTITY.md` definition 8 computes the same-being merge from the verdict V = Same. Calling that EVA verdict "the identity" is the conflation. RULED by the owner, 2026-11-20 ("go", on four points put to the owner): (1) the identity is a DEF (declared, resting on priors, naming asker, tolerance and giver), EVA asserts the evidence stands under it, REC revises it or applies the merge. (2) CELLS: a standing prior at DEF·Pattern (the asker, the tolerance, the giver and the priors it rests on) and a per-pair declaration at DEF·Figure that cites it; EVA·Figure and REC·Figure stay as they are. The Penelope session confirms and implements it; Janus writes none of it.
   RULED (3), givers: an identity learned from a corpus alone is at most a CANDIDATE (the grain theorem); a GIVEN needs a named giver (the asker, a treebank, a standard). RULED (4), models: a model may HOLD the universe in a long context and report the differences it finds with their witnessing addresses (a fold engine producing structure); it never judges sameness and never writes the identity DEF or the verdict, which stay Penelope's under the 2026-11-16 standing rule.
11. **All language passes through its codec to EOT; matching and reasoning happen in EOT, never on the surface** (the owner, 2026-11-20: "all languages must pass through a codec to EOT"; the standing law, `native/organs/nl-eot.mjs`: NL -> the language's UD grammar -> EOT -> back). The meaning layer (khora `eot-rich.js`) has content nodes, cube-addressed relations and features, function words absorbed as markers, NO word order and NO surface form. A language with no codec is a TYPED GAP (`no_grammar`, `codec_vendoring`), never a guess and never English by default. Any arm that ranks, merges or matches on surface strings (an anchor string, a lemma key, an embedding of raw sentences) is labelled SURFACE, whatever else it uses; an arm that reads the codec's rows but keeps string handles is GRAMMAR; only an arm whose nodes, relations and handles are EOT content nodes and cube-addressed relations may be called EOT. The harness registry `experiments/context-nomination/eot_law.py` declares the level of every arm, and `test_guard_alias.py` refuses an arm with no declared level, refuses an EOT label while no extractor reads through `nl-eot.mjs`, and requires every extractor to take a language code. Janus's own organs (`native/organs/*`) work on EOT-level inputs and import nothing that reads raw text.
12. **The codec is computed, not chosen, and every EOT record says which derivation made it** (the owner, 2026-11-20: "instead of having a discrete codec, compute it based on what we've learned in the past, along with givers ... from language to images"; DESIGN-COMPUTED-CODEC.md). `native/organs/codec-for.mjs` `codecFor(context)` selects, from `priors/codec-sources.json`, the learned SOURCES whose scope (material, language, variety, period) covers the context, weights them, loads each through the codec door and returns weighted LAYERS plus a `CodecDerivation@1` (sources, givers, scope, weights, standing, data hashes, gaps, a deterministic id). A source with no named giver, no scope or no data hash is refused at load; learned sources are standing `candidate` (a corpus can refute and never earn), `given` only with a named giver. A context no source covers is the typed gap `no_codec_source`: never another language's or period's codec and never an English hub; an unknown period is a hole said in the derivation. Every EOT record read through a layer carries the derivation id, and `requireDerivation` refuses one without. Default composition is the ENSEMBLE (layers); a BLEND is deferred until measured against it. Guarded by `native/organs/codec-for.test.mjs` and `native/organs/janus-rules.test.mjs`. Not yet done: a second English source (GUM would need the owner's word for a download), non-text materials (the image, audio and video adapters were not read), and wiring `codecFor` into the extractors frozen under the claim-graph and GAP pre-registrations (they use `loadGrammar` directly and are not changed).
13. **A tokenizer is a declared source with a named giver, scoped to a language and variety, falsified, and revisable; a library is allowed on those terms** (the owner, 2026-11-20: tokenizer libraries may be used as needed but need stated givers and must always be revisable; it must also work on creoles and non-standard English, so no English rule is ever applied to another language or variety). A tokenizer rule is a DEF·Pattern entry (`tokenizer-rule`: language, variety, period, effect, form, giver, standing, evidence) and a library enters the same way (its name, version, licence and hash as its giver). Janus OBSERVES and writes counts at Structure cells (`clitic-attachment` SEG·Pattern; `clitic-firing` CON·Figure, measured on a HELD-OUT corpus); Penelope DEFs the rule and writes the falsification verdict as an EVA·Pattern entry (`rule-verdict`: survived, refuted, underexposed or unexposed, up to a stated alpha; a corpus can refute and never earn, so a learned rule is at most `candidate`); the latest verdict stands, so a rule is revised by a newer entry. A rule is IN FORCE only when its latest verdict is `survived`; every other rule stays as data and touches no text. A variety or language with no surviving rule gets the typed gap `no_clitic_rules`, never the English ones. Derived features (`derivation-rule`, DEF·Pattern, structural and string-free, scoped by language, never overwriting a parser feature) are listed on every EOT record they touch. Guards: `native/organs/rule-entries.test.mjs`, `tokenizer-evidence.test.mjs`, `eot-read.test.mjs`. The measured case that motivates the falsifier: the learner kept `not` as a suffix rule on one treebank and it cuts "knot"; the held-out check marked it underexposed and it stays out of force.
14. **All work is done on the pharos; no system writes to itself or to another repo** (the owner, 2026-11-20). What one system learns reaches another only as entries on the shared log, written through its own door in the cells its role may write (Janus: Existence and Structure; Penelope: DEF, EVA, REC), and read back from the log. No script writes a prior or a registry that a reader then loads, and none writes into another repo. Guard: `native/organs/janus-rules.test.mjs` (the tokenizer and codec organs write no file and name no Interpretation cell; no self-written tokenizer prior exists). NOT YET MIGRATED, stated rather than hidden: `priors/codec-sources.json` (the codec source registry that `codec-for.mjs` reads, hand-authored here) and the khora-built `priors/ud-codecs.json` are files, not log entries; the experiment harnesses write result files under `~/Documents/janus-experiments` (outputs, not inputs to any reader).
15. **Nothing is lost: every reading can be reconstructed VERBATIM, and meaning units are chosen freely because of it** (the owner, 2026-11-20: "we can always reconstruct back to verbatim, so we don't lose anything"). The units of the codec are the units of MEANING the giver conveys (syntactic words, clitics, derived features), never a model's subword tokens (a model's tokens are internal to the model). That is safe only because the surface is kept: every record carries its sentence span, each token's span (UTF-16 units) and the gap of text that preceded it, so `reconstruct(records)` returns the source exactly and `verifyVerbatim` refuses a reader that cannot. Measured before it was fixed: the first layered reader stored each sentence's text but dropped the separators between sentences (6 characters lost in a test with spaces, a double newline and a tab), and `surfaceBytes()` returned an empty string; it now passes the round trip on irregular spacing, tabs, newlines, accents, an emoji and Arabic, with and without a clitic split. In the log, the exact text also lives in the `text` entry khora's reader writes. Guard: `native/organs/eot-read.test.mjs` (the verbatim tests, including a lossy-reader mutant). Not yet covered: `nl-eot.mjs` `read` (the frozen reader used by the GAP and claim-graph harnesses) still drops the gaps; it is frozen, so a caller who needs verbatim uses `eot-read.mjs`.

## What a new experiment must contain (the template)
Referent object. Evidence type and its giver. Stopping rule and the tolerance, with the sweep. Strings-only twin for every arm. Strata by the relation between mention and referent, and the in-document-bridge split. Controls (random, target-swap, locality). Falsifiers written first, including one that says the structure adds nothing over the twin. DEV, freeze, fresh once.
