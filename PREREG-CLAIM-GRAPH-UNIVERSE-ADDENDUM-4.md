## 1. What changes (and only this)
- NODES are the EOT content nodes. Determiners, case markers, auxiliaries, copulas, subordinators and punctuation are not nodes.
- EDGES are the EOT arcs (relation label kept with its cube cell); the gate reads `arc.rel` through the void's relation definition of AD-2 unchanged, and the copular predicate nominal is read from the head node's absorbed `cop` MARKER (not from a copula token).
- The CLASS handle (the merge of a link's mentions) is the sorted set of the content-node lemmas of the mention, not the surface anchor string; a mention with no content node keeps its surface key and is counted. Classes with the same content lemmas merge; the gold (link targets from the page bytes) and the DISJOINT stratum are recomputed on these classes and the gold counts are reported.
- SENTENCES that an embedder sees are the sentence's content-node lemma set (sorted, unique, no order); the class's anchor text for A0 is its lemma set. The embedder stays a given from its training (a nominator): the representation it is shown is EOT-level, the model is not.
- BINDINGS are recomputed on EOT: anaphors are pronoun nodes (PronType Prs) and definite-description nodes (a head noun carrying a `det` marker with Definite=Def); candidates are the class nodes in the same sentence and the previous 3 sentences (window 3, declared as before), excluded when the cube-addressed Number or Gender features of the two both exist and differ; all candidates listed, none chosen. Document order inside a sentence does not exist in EOT, so direction and distance within a sentence are not used.
- KINDS (admits) are induced by the same khora organ over EOT relation profiles (dep label of the node's arc, governor lemma, dependents' labels), unchanged otherwise.
- Everything else is the same code (ctxvoid.py, ctxadmit.py) run on the EOT graph, with the same arms, twins, controls, thresholds and falsifiers (H1 to H9, K1 to K4). The hop caps and TAU of NG and ADJF are as frozen.
