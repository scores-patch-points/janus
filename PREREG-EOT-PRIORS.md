# PREREG: do priors mined from the EOT of past confident readings resolve what the structural priors could not? (DRAFT until stamped, nothing scored)
Written 2026-11-20 at the owner's direction ("are we looking at the EOT parsed content of past confident readings?", "make it so"). PREREG-PRIOR-COLLAPSE and PREREG-UNIVERSES (both RESULTs on file) showed that the five structural priors (UPOS, relation, head UPOS, direction, distance, crossing) mined from gold arcs choose between the teacher's arc and ours about two-thirds of the time, cannot find the teacher's errors, and give universe weights no evidence. Those priors are GRAMMAR level and carry no lexical content. This test gives the priors content, at EOT level. Rules: REFERENT-FIRST.md (11 codec to EOT, 12 computed, 13 declared sources, 14 pharos). Stamp: sha256 first 16 hex in PREREG-EOT-PRIORS.md.sha256short, written before any scoring.

## 1. Sources (each with its giver; the counts are standing `candidate`, revisable)
- GOLD: EWT train + GUM train (treebanks as giver, the same 23,858 sentences as before).
- SILVER-AGREED: the 19,118 Wikipedia sentences annotated by the teacher (Stanza 1.15.1, giver) at `silver/teacher.conllu`, keeping ONLY the arcs where the teacher and our frozen POOL parser agree on head and relation (the confident readings). These are nominations: a second eye agreeing, not truth. Disclosed bias: agreement favours what our parser already does, so silver cannot teach us what we get wrong except through the lexical contexts it shares with gold; this is what L4 tests.
- Every record is built through EOT: the sentence rows go through khora `toEot` and the record is read from the EOT meaning layer (content nodes, absorbed markers, arcs), not from the rows. A candidate arc is scored the same way: a copy of the sentence with that candidate's head and relation is passed through `toEot`.

## 2. The EOT arc record and the tables
Record of a token's arc: dependent node (lemma, UPOS), its absorbed MARKERS' lemmas as a sorted set (case, mark, aux, cop, det, cc: absorbed by EOT, so word order and the dropped determiner do not matter), relation base, head node (lemma, UPOS). An absorbed token's record has itself as the marker and its host as the head. Four tables (counts, add-one smoothing, unseen context abstains because both candidates then score the same): E1 P(rel, head lemma+UPOS | dependent lemma+UPOS+markers); E2 P(rel, dependent lemma+UPOS+markers | head lemma+UPOS); E3 P(rel, head lemma+UPOS | dependent UPOS+markers); E4 P(rel, dependent UPOS+markers | head lemma+UPOS). E3 and E4 are the class back-offs.

## 3. Arms, each beside its twin (the same delta tokens, margins swept 0 0.5 1 2 3 4, as PREREG-PRIOR-COLLAPSE)
STRUCT: the five structural priors (replicated). SURFACE: tables E1 to E4 keyed on lower-cased word strings, with the linear previous token in place of the markers, no lemma, no UPOS (labelled SURFACE in eot_law). EOT-GOLD: E1 to E4 from GOLD only. EOT: from GOLD + SILVER-AGREED. COMBINED: STRUCT + EOT. Twins: EOT-SHUF, the same tables with the relation labels permuted as before ((i*7+3) mod R); and ALWAYS-TEACHER on the same tokens, reported beside every arm. Sets: LinES+ParTUT (REUSED), EWT dev, GUM dev (DEV). No FRESH claim. One run.

## 4. Falsifiers, written first (precision on collapsed reachable tokens; coverage reported; a precision difference counts only at coverage no lower than the comparator's at the same margin, else it is reported as a trade)
L1: COMBINED at m = 1 is not above STRUCT by 3 points. L2: EOT is not above SURFACE by 2 points (EOT adds nothing over strings). L3: EOT is not above EOT-SHUF by 5 points (content carries nothing relation-specific). L4: EOT is not above EOT-GOLD by 1 point (past confident readings add nothing over gold). L5: COMBINED at m = 1 is not above ALWAYS-TEACHER on the same tokens (still cannot beat believing the teacher). L6 (veto): where COMBINED prefers OURS at m = 1, ours is right in under 50% of those tokens (it still cannot find the teacher's errors).
Honest reading: if L1, L2, L3, L4 are NOT hit, content-bearing EOT priors from past confident readings help where structural ones could not; L5 and L6 say whether they could replace or check the teacher. If L5 and L6 are hit, they remain a nomination and a spectrum weight, never a verdict. Standard English only; no claim for creoles; no claim beyond the delta tokens of these sets.
