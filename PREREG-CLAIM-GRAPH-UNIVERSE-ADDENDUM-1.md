# ADDENDUM-1 to PREREG-CLAIM-GRAPH-UNIVERSE (parent stamp 1a37523f89f6567a): the universe is the referent's connections to ALL other nodes, grown until they stop mattering. Declared BEFORE any fresh read and BEFORE the full-graph arms were scored on anything
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session at its user's correction: "its not just about referents mentioned, its about their connections with all other nodes until such point as those connections are not relevant". The parent's CG arm was seeded by the class's own string and grew only through clauses where a MENTION or a lemma-keyed argument is a core argument (UD nsubj, obj, iobj, obl, csubj, ccomp, xcomp): most of the document's connections (modifiers, appositions, conjuncts, copular complements, the whole tree) were not edges, and on DEV 317 of 416 classes never grew. That was a thin universe, not the principle. The parent is unchanged and its CG arms are still reported as CG_core.

## 1. The full graph (new arms; fullgraph.mjs extracts, ctxfull.py grows and scores)
- NODE: every reader token. Merged into one node: all tokens of an anchor class's mention spans (handle 1: the anchor string) and all NOUN or PROPN tokens that share a reader lemma (handle 2: the lemma, declared). Every other token is its own node. Function words stay as nodes and stay connected; nothing is dropped by a word list.
- EDGE: every dependency relation the reader emits, whatever its label (undirected), so the connections of a referent include every modifier, apposition, conjunct, predicate and argument path, within and (through merged nodes) across sentences. FGB adds Janus's binding candidates as edges (anaphor token to each candidate class, number mismatch excluded, none chosen).
- UNIVERSE of a class at hop h = the class's own sentences plus the sentences of every node within graph distance h of the class node. Sentences are kept as units; a candidate class scores by its best cosine to any universe sentence other than its own.
- RELEVANCE = the stopping rule, unchanged: the reach is the smallest h at which the top-10 shares at least TAU = 0.8 of its members with the previous distinct universe (TAU swept 0.6 and 1.0); a hop that adds no sentence is skipped. HMAXF = 8 is a safety cap only (a run still growing at 8 is counted unstable). The reach is thus the point at which the next ring of connections no longer changes what the asker would be told, not a number of hops.

## 2. Controls, each beside its arm (rule 3) and the falsifiers
A0 (bare anchor), T_FG (same hop-0 sentences plus random sentences to the size FG reached), ADJF (adjacency universe, same cap and rule), PROX (locality), FLATF (the graph with every sentence's head pointers permuted: structure removed, strings and merges kept), FG_NOLEM (only anchor classes merge: it says what the lemma handle contributes), target-swap. Falsifiers: G1 FG not above A0; G2 not above T_FG; G3 not above ADJF; G4 not above PROX; G5 not above FLATF; G6 swap ratio >= 0.5; G7 FGB not above FG; G8 precision@10 under half of A0; G9 FG not above FG_NOLEM. Same thresholds as the parent (document-bootstrap interval above 0 and a difference of at least 0.05 recall@10). Primary stratum DISJOINT; GROWN_DISJOINT is co-reported (and judged where it holds 30 gold). A pass requires G2 to G5 NOT hit.

## 3. Declared before scoring, and what is not claimed
The extraction and these arms were written after the parent's DEV run, so DEV is REUSED and no DEV number is claimed; the first run on DEV is a debugging run. The honest limit of this design: both handles (anchor string and lemma) are strings, the graph is the parser's one reading of English, and the embedder is a given from its training: the arms nominate, they never say two classes are the same being. A positive result is that structure over ALL of a referent's connections nominates its aliases better than the same strings; a negative one names which part (structure, lemma handle, reach, extraction) failed.
