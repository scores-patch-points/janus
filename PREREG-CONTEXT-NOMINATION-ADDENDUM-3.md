## 1. Why
The parent's profile arms cut the referent's universe with constants (the first K = 5 name-bearing sentences, a window of 3): arbitrary constants, labelled as such in the harness. The principle (khora identity-induction.js; THEORY-IDENTITY definition 12) is that a referent's universe widens until the next step makes no difference at the asker's tolerance. This addendum adds an arm that uses that STOPPING RULE, and the controls that say whether its structure adds anything over its own strings.

## 2. Arms and controls added (all nominators; none judges)
- N6 (the universe by a stopping rule): seeds = ALL name-bearing sentences of the document (not the first K). Hop h = every sentence within document distance h of a seed (h = 0 is the seeds). Each profile sentence is kept as a UNIT: a candidate's score is its best cosine to any profile sentence (a sentence never matches itself; no averaging, no truncation of a concatenation). The reach is the smallest h >= 1 whose top-10 shares at least TAU = 0.8 of its members with the top-10 of hop h - 1 (at most 2 of 10 nominations differ: the declared tolerance). HMAX = 6 is a safety cap only (a run that reaches it is counted as unstable). TAU is swept and reported at 0.6 and 1.0 (arms N6_t06, N6_t10); the primary is 0.8, fixed BEFORE any DEV run of this arm. The name-bearing sentences are excluded from every ranking, as for every arm.
- N6F = N6 where a universe exists (the name appears in at least one sentence), otherwise N0, as N5 is to N3.
- T6, the strings-only TWIN of N6: the same seeds plus sentences drawn at random (seeded) from the document to the same profile size N6 reached, scored identically. T6F = T6 where seeds exist, else N0.
- PROX, a locality control with no model: sentences ranked by document distance to the nearest seed (ties by order). PROXF = PROX where seeds exist, else N0. It says whether N6 is the referent's universe or only proximity to its mentions.
- The harness registry (ARM_SPECS in experiments/context-nomination/ctxnom.py, guarded by check_registry and test_guard.py) refuses to report a context arm that declares no referent object, evidence, twin and cut, and prints beside every result which arms rest on an ARBITRARY CONSTANT (N1, N2, N3, N5): their results do not speak for the full universe.

## 3. Falsifiers added (thresholds and intervals as F1: a document-bootstrap interval of the paired difference above 0 and a difference of at least 0.05 recall@10 on ZERO-CLEAN)
F1c: N6F does not beat N0. F7: N6F does not beat T6F (the structure adds nothing over the same number of strings). F8: N6F does not beat PROXF (it is locality, not the universe). F2 also applies to N6 and N6F (target-swap ratio 0.5 or more is a hit). The claim "a referent-bounded universe nominates better than its own strings" stands only if F7 and F8 are NOT hit for N6F.

## 4. What DEV showed, and the expectation stated now (DEV numbers are not claimed)
On DEV the arm as built did NOT pass its controls: within the 28 ZERO-CLEAN gold whose name appears (a stratum under 30, unjudgeable), N6 0.179 against its twin T6 0.143, locality PROX 0.286 and the profile query N3 0.500; pooled, F1c, F7 and F8 were all hit and N6's target-swap ratio was 1.00. The reading, stated before the fresh read and not a result: widening through DOCUMENT ADJACENCY is locality, not the referent's universe, because a referent's universe is its claims, bonds and neighbours (a graph), not the sentences around its mentions. So F1c, F7, F8 and the N6 swap hit are EXPECTED on FRESH-2, and a hit is the finding that adjacency is the wrong proxy, not that the principle is wrong. The arm was NOT tuned to pass: TAU was fixed before its DEV run and nothing about it was changed after.
The experiment that tests the principle properly grows the universe through the claim graph (the reader's dependency rows to claims, the beings that co-participate in them, Janus's bonds and bindings, each hop kept only if it is upstream of the asker's concern) with the same stopping rule, the same twin and the same locality control. It needs claim extraction on every document and is NOT run here; it is the next pre-registration.
