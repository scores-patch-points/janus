# PREREG: libraries as TEACHERS: can a library's parses on raw text make our own parser better, and what do they not help? (frozen before any teacher output is used; nothing run)
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session at its user's direction ("we should use these partly as teachers to get better and better, knowing they wont help with creoles"). Parent: PREREG-PARSE-LIBRARIES (stamp 02c3fbc2bddf0d59: the library arms and falsifiers L1 to L3 on the fresh sets) and PREREG-PARSE-ENGLISH-LAYERS (our frozen parsers). Stamp: PREREG-PARSE-TEACHERS.md.sha256short.

## 1. The idea, and the standing it gives a teacher
A teacher is a SOURCE (codec-sources style: library name and version, model package, file hashes, licence caveats, a named giver, standing candidate) whose parses of RAW TEXT become SILVER training examples for our own parser. A silver tree is a nomination, never gold: it carries its teacher in the file (a `# source =` comment per sentence), it is stored locally only (the weights derive from data that includes non-commercial GUM: research use, never committed), and nothing about it is `given`. The student is our own parser family (khora's train-ud-language.mjs recipe, the one that made POOL): the teacher teaches it; the teacher is never run in our pipeline's decisions. Where no teacher exists the answer is the typed gap `no_teacher`: creoles are that case, stated now (the registry has Naija and Haitian Creole treebanks as givers; no library model is assumed or checked for them here); our emergent signals (retroactive surprise, tagger confidence, the ants) and those treebanks are the route there, and the teacher recipe is language-neutral so a teacher is used wherever one covers the variety and never elsewhere.

## 2. Material
UNLABELLED text: the raw sentences of the Wikipedia articles already on this machine from the earlier experiments (data/dev and data/fresh2 of the context-nomination work: 90 articles, CC BY-SA, never seen by any parser of ours as training and not part of any UD treebank): tokenized and tagged and parsed by the teacher with ITS OWN tokenizer (so the silver trees also carry the clitic split as the UD convention has it). Gold for the student: EWT train + GUM train (exactly POOL's data). Evaluation: FRESH sets LinES test and ParTUT test (read once each, gold tokenization given, the same metrics and document bootstrap as the parents); EWT and GUM test are REUSED (read once for the frozen arms) and PUD test is fresh for the student but not for the teacher (it trained on PUD): reported with that flag.

## 3. Arms (one recipe, one seed per arm: khora train-ud-language.mjs, cap = every sentence, 10 iterations, no lexicon, as POOL)
POOL (frozen, gold only). STUDENT_T = gold + ALL the teacher's silver. STUDENT_SELF (the twin): gold + silver trees from OUR OWN pooled parser on the SAME sentences and the SAME tokens (self-training: if this matches STUDENT_T the teacher adds nothing beyond more text). STUDENT_SHUF (the label-destroyed twin): gold + the teacher's silver with the heads permuted within each sentence (seeded). The teacher itself (STANZA) is reported as the ceiling the student is trying to approach, not as an arm of ours.

## 4. Falsifiers, written first (LAS points; the 0.5-point size and the interval above 0 as in the parents)
- T1: STUDENT_T does not beat POOL on LinES test AND ParTUT test (teaching adds nothing on text no one trained on).
- T2: STUDENT_T does not beat STUDENT_SELF on both (the gain, if any, is not the teacher's: more text alone did it).
- T3: STUDENT_SHUF is not worse than POOL by 0.5 on both (the student is insensitive to what the silver trees say: the training is not learning from the teacher's structure).
- T4 (reported, no gate): STUDENT_T's LAS as a share of the teacher's on the fresh sets; the per-relation gains (does the student gain on the long-range relations where our errors concentrate: conj, advcl, acl, obl, compound).
- Reading rule: teachers are worth using as teachers only if T1 AND T2 are NOT hit and T3 is NOT hit. A hit names what the silver did not do. No claim beyond English, standard text, this parser family, and one silver corpus of this size (about 19 thousand sentences): a larger or better-chosen silver set is a separate pre-registered step. Provenance and standing carry through: every silver file names its teacher; the student is a CANDIDATE source in the registry, judged by Penelope, with the teacher's claims about its own training data recorded as the giver's statement.

## 5. Rules this follows
Rule 11: the teacher reads raw text through ITS codec to UD rows; our student is trained on UD rows and decodes to EOT as before. Rule 13/14: nothing is written to any log by this experiment; adoption (a student as a codec source, silver as evidence) is entries through the door judged by Penelope. A model never judges: a teacher parse is a reading with a source, and the student is judged by the frozen protocol, not by the teacher.
