# PREREG: does a small language model, read through a likelihood gate, know what the structural and EOT priors do not? (DRAFT until stamped, nothing scored)
Written 2026-11-20 at the owner's direction ("use a 2b model ... we don't expect perfect json ... use physics to gate it ... and/or Penelope", "lets try it"). Builds on RESULT-EOT-PRIORS: content priors from EOT lift the collapse to 65-67% but cannot beat the teacher (80-84%) or find its errors (ours right 20-29%). Rules: REFERENT-FIRST.md (13: a model is a declared source with a named giver, revisable; Janus counts, Penelope judges). Stamp: sha256 first 16 hex in PREREG-LLM-GATE.md.sha256short, before any scoring.

## 1. The source and the gate
- Giver: gemma2:2b (Q4_0, 2.6B parameters, Ollama, digest 8ccf136fdd52, already local, nothing downloaded). A codec source and a SECOND EYE, never a voter. Temperature 0, one output token.
- The disputed token (teacher and our POOL parser disagree, the gold equals one of them: the same REACHABLE delta tokens as PREREG-PRIOR-COLLAPSE) is posed as a two-way question: the pretokenized sentence, the word and its position, and the two candidate readings (A: depends on the teacher's head word with the teacher's relation; B: the same for ours; the relation names are UD labels as the parsers write them, a root head is written "the root of the sentence"). Each token is asked in BOTH orders.
- THE GATE (no JSON): the model never writes a structure. The gate is the model's own log-probability of the letter for each reading, read from the top log-probs of the single output token; a letter not in the top list scores -20. d = the mean over both orders of log2 P(teacher's letter) - log2 P(ours). This is a spectrum weight in bits, held and collapsed only at use, exactly as the priors' margin. A free-text answer is not used; where the model's letter is not among the top log-probs the pick is HELD, never parsed.
- Penelope: Janus writes the counts (picks, margins, agreement of the two orders). The verdict on any rule drawn from this (for instance "a 2B model's preference at margin m is a standing-candidate weight") is Penelope's; nothing here convicts.

## 2. Arms (same tokens, margins swept 0 0.5 1 2 3 bits)
LLM (with the sentence); LLM-NOSENT, the twin: the same two readings with the sentence and position removed (only the word and the two head words and relations: what it guesses from word frequency alone); EOT, the frozen priors of PREREG-EOT-PRIORS (read from their dump); LLM+EOT, the two margins added (both in log2 units); always-teacher on the same tokens beside every arm. Sets: LinES+ParTUT (REUSED), EWT dev and GUM dev (DEV). Disclosed: the model may have seen these treebanks in pretraining; the contamination cannot be measured here, so none of these is a FRESH claim.

## 3. Falsifiers, written first
M1: LLM at full coverage (m = 0) is not above EOT by 3 points. M2: LLM is not above LLM-NOSENT by 5 points (it does not read the sentence). M3 (veto): where LLM prefers OURS at |d| >= 1, ours is right in under 50% of those tokens (it still cannot find the teacher's errors). M4: LLM+EOT is not above the better of LLM and EOT by 2 points at m = 0. M5: the two orders pick differently on more than 30% of tokens (order bias, an unreliable gate). M6: LLM does not beat always-teacher on the same tokens at any margin of at least 1 bit with coverage of at least 20%. M7 (calibration): precision at |d| >= 2 is not at least 5 points above precision at |d| < 0.5 (the margin is not evidence).
Honest reading: if M1, M2, M3 and M7 are NOT hit, the model carries sentence-specific knowledge the priors lack and its margin is usable as a weight; M3 is the one that has failed every time. If M3 and M6 are hit it is a weight and a nomination, never a replacement.
