# DESIGN (a proposal for the owner and khora; nothing here is built): image and music codecs that turn the material into EOT by discovering its meaningful structure, interoperable with the text codecs through the one envelope
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session at its user's direction: "to really know that we're doing this right, we should also have the image and music codecs working and make it all interoperable ... this isn't like an MP4 style codec; this is turning everything into EOT, where we run our system to find what the meaningful structure is and encode that into EOT; music would be finding beats and voices, not processing the bytes per se". Builds on DESIGN-COMPUTED-CODEC.md and REFERENT-FIRST rules 11 and 12. Every statement about what exists was checked on 2026-11-20 against the file named; what I did not read is listed.

## 1. What exists (checked)
- Charter (khora native/docs/THE-ISTHMUS.md): the codec table lists, for image or screen, decode `adapters/image/screen-read.js` and encode "none yet"; for audio, video, midi, math and code, "none yet". So NO non-text material has a codec that yields EOT, and none has an encode path.
- audio (`adapters/audio/reading.js`): the perceiver turns PCM into per-frame field vectors (chroma, timbre, moments); its header says it does NO structure finding: "onsets, beats, segmentation are emergence's". The structure finder is khora's emergence engine (`legacy-ported/packages/engine/emergence/`: activation, binding, surprise, tiers; tiers.js: meaning folds on itself, each tier's prior is what the tier below's surprises accumulate into). I read only tiers.js's header; I did not read how its outputs (segments, beats) are shaped, and the legacy-ported copy is not in the khora checkout I hold (it is on khora main).
- midi (`adapters/midi/midi.js`): addressed note events (ordinal and tick) in and out; khora's own note calls a MIDI file "a script", "the cleanest possible material", pure, no music theory, pitch as the file states it, a refusal with its reason for a file that does not read back. `adapters/build/music-medium.js` is a build pipeline that snips bars from a licensed score and continues with sedimented priors: it is not a decoder into EOT.
- image (`adapters/image/material.js`, `screen-read.js`): luminance per scanline, and a screenshot reader that returns a measured model (boxes, rules, image regions, OCR text); its thresholds are "set by hand ... not measured" by its own admission. video: motion energy per transition.
- Therefore: perceivers (signal to field vectors, boxes, note events) exist; structure discovery exists as an engine; the EOT meaning layer for these materials, its relation vocabulary, its givers, its derivation records and its encode path do not.

## 2. What "a codec into EOT" means for these materials
1. DECODE = discover, then address. Run the system's own structure finders over the perceiver's output (never the bytes' format): for music, onsets and their periodicity (beats), grouping (bars), voices (streams of notes that belong together), repeats and variants (motifs); for an image, regions, containment, adjacency, alignment, reading order, repeated elements. Each discovered thing is a CONTENT NODE; each relation between nodes is cube-addressed; features carry their cell; the SURFACE LAYER is only an address back into the material (a tick range, a frame range, a pixel box), never content. The meaning layer holds no sample, no pixel and no byte order, exactly as the text layer holds no word order.
2. STANDING of what is discovered: a discovered beat or voice is a NOMINATION with a null (the shuffled-onset or shuffled-pixel control, the khora way: a surprise measured against a null, not a threshold set by hand) and standing `candidate`; a declared convention (the MIDI 1.0 spec, scientific pitch notation, a time signature a score states) is `given` with its named giver. A discovered structure never becomes given by being found.
3. THE RELATION VOCABULARY needs a giver per material, as UD is for language: e.g. precedes, simultaneous, contains, repeats, variant-of, voice-of, aligned-with, adjacent-to, rendered-as. Each is addressed in the cube (the existing `addressOfRelation` mechanism, extended by declared entries), with the giver named. Where a relation is only discovered, it is a candidate entry, not a given one.
4. ENCODE = from the meaning layer alone, through the codec's own learned parameters (for music: the measured durations, ordering and pitch-class statistics of the sources, as UDGrammar@1 holds word-order parameters), back to the material, with the round trip measured in the three levels the text codec already uses (eot-rich.js): L1 the surface layer re-serializes to the exact bytes, L2 the meaning layer alone rebuilds the discovered structure, L3 the material is regenerated from structure plus learned parameters and its loss is measured and stated.
5. EVERY record carries its derivation (rule 12): which structure finders, which perceiver, which sources and givers, which weights. `codecFor` is generalized from text to a material: a music codec is itself computed from the sources of learned structure (corpora, goldens, givers) and the context (instrument, tempo, genre if known), and a material with no source is the typed gap `no_codec_source`.

## 3. Interoperability, defined so that it can fail
- One envelope: every codec returns layers plus a `CodecDerivation@1`; every EOT record names its derivation; `requireDerivation` refuses one without it. This is built for text (codec-for.mjs) and is the shared door.
- No modality branch above the codec: the organs that match, fold, derive and contradict (Janus's gate and evidence organs, Penelope's DEF, EVA and REC) take EOT and contain no check of what material it came from. A guard scans them for modality names and fails on one.
- A mixed graph: a text record, a music record and an image record sit in one EOT graph and one relation query runs over all three with the same code. First concrete form: an image region whose OCR text goes through the English codec (its EOT is linked to the region node), and a sung line whose lyric goes through the text codec and whose melody goes through the music codec, linked by `rendered-as` and `simultaneous`.
- Cross-modal structure: the same relation (repeats, returns, contains) found in text, music and image records is matched by one operator. A control is required: the matcher run on shuffled relations must score near the null.

## 4. A first slice that can be built and tested with what we hold, and its falsifiers (to pre-register, not run)
SLICE A, MIDI to EOT and back. Material: Standard MIDI files (the cleanest: events are declared, nothing is pitch-tracked). Decode: tracks and channels give declared voices; beats from the onset periodicity with a null; bars from beat grouping; repeats from self-similarity of note contours; each with its candidate standing and its null. Encode: regenerate note events from the meaning layer and the learned parameters. Falsifiers: F1 the discovered beat period does not beat the shuffled-onset null; F2 discovered voices agree with the file's own tracks no better than random partitions of the same sizes; F3 repeats found in the original do not survive a transposition and tempo change (a structure that is the bytes, not the music); F4 the L2 round trip loses notes (the structure is not a representation); F5 the mixed-graph query on shuffled relations scores like the true one (the interoperability is decoration). A corpus of public-domain MIDI is needed; I have not checked which we hold locally, and any download needs the owner's word with its source and size named.
SLICE B, an image or screenshot with text: screen-read regions to EOT nodes and relations (contains, adjacent, aligned, reading order), the OCR text through `codecFor` (so the text half uses the derivation envelope), the regions' relations measured against a null. It inherits the hand-set thresholds of screen-read.js, which must be replaced or measured before any claim.
SLICE C, audio (PCM): the emergence engine over the perceiver's field vectors to onsets, beats and voices. The hardest (voices from timbre and chroma) and last; it needs me to read emergence/binding.js and activation.js first.
