# ERRATUM-1 to PREREG-ANT-COLONY-SURPRISE and ADDENDUM-1 (stamps 8465cb986b159817 and 7f5fe2ea1e0dfbb9): the tagger-confidence score is MISCALIBRATED; the conclusions drawn from it are WITHDRAWN
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session after the Penelope session's noise calibration (her experiment experiments/self-supervised-segmentation, PREREG-CALIBRATION f6e44c4e3e4b1ff5) and my own check of the colony's score (experiments/ants/confcheck.mjs; hash in the freeze file's directory listing).
## What was checked
For every word-final cut candidate (455) on a 1,000-sentence sample of the learning stream, the mean tagger-margin change over the sentences the cut changes, scored two ways: FULL (the colony's: all tokens, new pieces included) and CTX (context tokens only), a candidate being GOOD if its observed change beats the MAXIMUM of 30 random same-length cuts on the same sentences. By chance at most 1/31 = 3.2% should be good. On REAL text: FULL good 54 of 410 (13.2%), CTX good 30 of 410 (7.3%). On NOISE (every token's characters shuffled, lengths kept): FULL good 32 of 206 (15.5%), CTX good 26 of 206 (12.6%). The score passes noise as often as it passes real text, and `'s` passes on noise too. A first, smaller check (100 candidates, 300 sentences; 33 scored on noise) had shown 0 good and was too small to see it.
## What is withdrawn
ARM ANT_CONF, RAND_CONF and the falsifiers A6, A7, A8 (all resting on the tagger-confidence score) are WITHDRAWN as evidence: the score cannot tell structure from noise at the single-cut level, so "the ants rediscovered cut:'s on the competency score" is NOT supported, and the KNOWN program's confidence (+4.61 against a null of -11.2) is not evidence either. The statement in the run report that ants plus a competency signal can rediscover the clitic rule is withdrawn.
## What stands, and what does not carry over
The code-length arms (ANT, ANT_NOGUARD, RAND) used the search-aware ceiling of a character-PERMUTED copy of the stream, which is the noise null itself, so A1 to A5 stand as reported (A1 hit on peak gain with trails changing the search: 86 programs beyond the ceiling against 2, wasted 34% against 94%; A2 hit with the guard untestable in a program language of cuts; A5 hit: compression-optimal slicing disagrees with the treebank's word units). The clitic-effect evidence on the log (LAS on the other tokens, the possessive recovery, wrongCuts, a random-cut null, bootstrap) is a different measure that excludes the new pieces by construction and is NOT affected. The competency signal the colony needs is therefore still missing: tagger margin is not it unless its bias is controlled (the Penelope session's context-only measure calibrates at 4.0 to 5.9% on her noise; mine does not reproduce that, 12.6%, so the controls differ and are not interchangeable). No colony entry has been written to any log.
