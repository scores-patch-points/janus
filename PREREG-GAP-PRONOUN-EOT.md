# PREREG: pronoun binding on GAP, with the text parsed into EOT: how often can structure COMMIT, how often is a commit right, and does EOT beat the reader's surface rows? (frozen before any arm touches GAP; nothing scored)
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session at its user's direction ("Yes, pre-register", after downloading GAP with the owner's word). Rules: REFERENT-FIRST.md (rule 4 a benchmark states what it measures, rule 10 DEF declares, rule 11 all language through its codec to EOT). Data: experiments/gap/DATA-GAP.md (sha256 of the three TSVs). Stamp: sha256 first 16 hex in PREREG-GAP-PRONOUN-EOT.md.sha256short.

## 0. Prior art, read first (rule 8)
khora native/docs/PRONOUN-BINDING-STATUS.md: the canonical presence binder on UD English GUM (125 documents, 1,299 answerable he/she pronouns): pooled precision 0.689 at about 51% coverage (chosen dials 0.547 at about 80%; random about 0.5); 69% of the wrong bindings bound a non-person; the binder's margin does not separate right from wrong; the chase resolver, embedding-window rulings and static smell were tried and not adopted. Penelope organs/anaphor-commit.mjs: "this pronoun refers to X" is a DEF at Figure; activation never decides; a candidate leaves only on a declared checkable fact (gender-clash, non-nominal, disjoint-reference); COMMIT iff exactly one survives, NEW if none, HELD otherwise. Janus's binding evidence lists every candidate and picks none. This experiment evaluates that division of labour on a benchmark the document answers; it does not build a new binder.

## 1. What GAP measures (rule 4, stated before use)
2,000 development, 454 validation and 2,000 test rows: a short Wikipedia passage, a pronoun (he, she, his, her, him, hers, himself, herself) at a character offset, two candidate names A and B at offsets, gold: the pronoun refers to A, to B, or to NEITHER (A-coref and B-coref both FALSE). The two candidates are usually the same gender BY DESIGN, so gender agreement cannot separate them in most rows. It measures: binding a pronoun to one of two named people from evidence inside the passage. It does NOT measure: binding over a whole document's cast, world knowledge, or any language but English.

## 2. Referent object, evidence, giver
The being: a candidate A or B, a person mention located by its character span (the asker's handle: the benchmark's offsets, used only to find the mention in the EOT nodes). Evidence: EOT records of the passage (nl-eot.mjs `read`, language eng; content nodes, cube-addressed arcs and features, absorbed markers). Janus lists, per pronoun, which of A and B remain candidates and the raw facts (cube-addressed Gender and Number agreement: match, mismatch or unknown; the arcs between pronoun and candidate: clause-mate, role). Janus chooses nothing. The COMMIT step is Penelope's declared ruling (gender-clash on cube features, non-nominal by upos, disjoint-reference by core arguments of one predicate); the harness MIRRORS those three rules over EOT records as a test double, labelled a mirror (standing candidate), never a DEF written to any log. Giver of the rules: Penelope's anaphor-commit.mjs, whose rows-level behaviour is the reference (section 5).

## 3. Arms (every arm outputs A, B, NEITHER or HELD; HELD is allowed and is not an error)
- EOT-J: Janus's EOT candidate list restricted to {A, B}; output A or B if one remains, NEITHER if none remains, HELD if both remain. (the structure facts, no further rule)
- EOT-P: EOT-J plus the mirrored rules; COMMIT = exactly one survivor: A or B; NEITHER if none; HELD if both. The headline arm.
- ROWS-P: the same rules over the reader's surface rows (pharos-reading readingRows, the GRAMMAR path, English), i.e. Penelope's organ as it runs today where the harness can call it, else the mirror over rows. It exists so EOT is compared with the level below it on the same rows (rule 11).
- Twins and baselines (rule 3): SHUF (EOT-P with the cube Gender and Number features and the core-argument arcs shuffled among the passage's nodes: structure destroyed, same strings); NEAR (the candidate nearest the pronoun by characters, a pure surface heuristic, always commits); FIRST (the first-mentioned candidate, always commits); RAND (a seeded coin between A and B, always commits). NEAR, FIRST and RAND commit on every row; they are the strings-only baselines for precision at full coverage.
- Guards (checks, not falsifiers): every arm's output is invariant when A and B are swapped and when the passage's candidate order is reversed (no order preference); no arm reads the gold; the harness imports nothing that writes a log.

## 4. Metrics and strata
Per arm: coverage = share of rows with a COMMIT (A, B or NEITHER); precision = correct commits / commits; net = (correct commits minus wrong commits) / rows (a commit that is wrong costs as much as a right one earns; HELD costs nothing). NEITHER is a commit and is scored against the gold NEITHER. Strata (judged only with at least 30 rows): by gold class (A, B, NEITHER); by pronoun gender (he-class, she-class); SEPARABLE (A and B differ in a cube Gender or Number feature, or in upos nominality) versus NOT-SEPARABLE; pronoun in the same sentence as the candidates versus a later sentence. Bootstrap by URL (rows from one article are not independent), 2,000 resamples, seed 20261119; a difference needs an interval above 0 and the stated size.

## 5. Falsifiers, written first
- GP1: EOT-P's precision on its COMMITTED rows does not exceed RAND's precision on the SAME rows by at least 0.10 (the rules add nothing over a coin where they commit).
- GP2: EOT-P's coverage is under 5% of rows (the structure almost never decides: a finding that GAP rows are not decided by these three facts, not a failure of the principle).
- GP3: net(EOT-P) minus net(ROWS-P) is not above 0 with an interval above 0 and a difference of at least 0.02 (EOT adds nothing over the reader's rows: the owner's hypothesis that EOT is how pronoun resolution improves is NOT supported on this task).
- GP4: net(EOT-P) minus net(SHUF) is not above 0 with an interval above 0 (the structure adds nothing over the same strings).
- GP5: EOT-P's wrong-commit rate (wrong commits / rows) is at or above NEAR's wrong-commit rate at full coverage when NEAR's precision exceeds EOT-P's (a rules-based abstainer that is both less accurate and not safer than a distance heuristic has no reason to exist).
- GP6 (agreement with the reference): on the rows where the harness can run Penelope's organ over the same reader rows, the mirror's decisions differ from the organ's on more than 5% of rows (the mirror is not a faithful test double; every difference is listed).
- A pass needs GP1 to GP5 NOT hit and GP6 not hit. A hit names the part that failed: the rules, the EOT features, the candidate framing, or the mirror.

## 7. What this does not test
Penelope writing the DEF (a DEF is hers; the mirror evaluates what her declared rules would commit); a model reading the passage (the 2026-11-16 ruling: a model may report differences with witnesses, never judge); other languages (typed gap: the codec for them is not exercised here); whole-document casts; the quality of the embedder (none is used).
