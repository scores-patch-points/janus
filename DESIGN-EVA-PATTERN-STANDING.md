# DESIGN (a proposal, for the Penelope session and the owner): EVA·Pattern, a claim's standing across independent witnesses
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session at its user's direction ("make it so": draft the EVA·Pattern design, and make Janus's evidence carry the source identity it needs). Nothing here is built or measured. Janus writes no EVA: EVA is Interpretation, which is Penelope's (pharos lib/writers.mjs). Every cell, number and behaviour below was checked against the file named beside it on 2026-11-20.

## 1. Why this EVA first
- In the log, EVA·Figure has one writer (`equivalence-verdict`); EVA·Ground and EVA·Pattern have none (pharos docs/PROPOSAL-WRITER-PHASEPOSTS.md, rows 31-33).
- The informed impostor (ledger row 27: a date-like parenthesis prepended passed 1.000 of verdict-bearing trials) forges surface text. A forged text is ONE witness. Standing across witnesses is the cell that can resist it, if the witnesses are independent.
- Independence is weak in practice: the ledger found the frames are not independent (row 4: effective 1.94 of 3 for French).

## 2. What exists (khora `native/organs/capacity-runner.js`, `mergeTestimony`, about line 850 onward)
A PURE function over per-source readings, no model, no new log landing. Five statuses: AGREE (two or more sources hold), DISAGREE (some hold, some refuse), SINGLE (exactly one holds, none refuse), UNDETERMINED (nothing holds or refuses), CONTRADICTED (every determining source refuses, none holds: a fifth case the spec's author found missing). A reading that READ NOTHING (the model's own weights, `self:model`, generalised structurally as `readsNothing`: an unaddressed hold) never counts toward AGREE; DISAGREE still reads the raw arrays, so a self-witness opposed by a real refusal is a real disagreement. Its stated reason: an unread assertion would manufacture standing that was never earned. The cube doc places this at EVA·Pattern (Paradigm, Tracing) and calls it "a property of the SET that no member carries".

## 3. What Janus now supplies (built, tested, local)
Every witness inside a Janus finding carries `source`: PROVENANCE given by the caller on the claim (a document address, a host). It is not a ground: a ground says where or when a claim holds and decides which claims meet; two documents must be comparable, so a document is never a ground. Every difference, agreement and incomparable hole carries `sameSource`: true, false, or null when either source is unknown (null, never false). The evidence carries `sources` (the distinct ones) and counts `agreementsCrossSource`, `agreementsSameSource`, `differencesCrossSource`; the merged world counts `sources`. These are plain structural facts, never a judgement. NOT supplied: whether two sources are mirrors of one another, the reading chain, who read.

## 4. The proposed organ (Penelope's): `standing`, EVA·Pattern
1. Per relation under a declaration's concerns, group the witnesses by `source`. A source counts once. Collapse declared mirrors (the Fold's A2 pre-registration F1: a page sharing 80% or more of its sentences with another is not a separate witness; the threshold is a declared value with a named giver, never a constant here).
2. Per relation, apply `mergeTestimony`'s statuses over the independent sources, excluding any reading that read nothing from AGREE.
3. The declaration-level assertion (the owner's ruling: EVA asserts the evidence STANDS under the declaration, only ever "up to"): the fold stands under identity-declaration D up to tolerance tau iff no relation in D's concerns is DISAGREE or CONTRADICTED beyond tau, and at least one relation is AGREE across two or more independent sources by a probe with power above 0. SINGLE and UNDETERMINED are gaps, never agreement.
4. Entry: kind `standing`, cell EVA·Pattern, addr `standing:<sha16 of pair and declaration>`, `of` = [the declaration id, the evidence ids it read], body { pair, under, up_to, per_relation: [{ rel, status, holds: [source], refuses: [source], silent: [source] }], independent_sources, excluded: [{ source, why: "reads_nothing" | "same_source" | "mirror" }] }. The writer is Penelope; a model-named writer or giver is refused, as for the identity entries.

## 5. To pre-register (falsifiers, in the repo's discipline)
(a) Strings-only twin: the same number of agreeing witnesses from ONE source must come out SINGLE, not AGREE; if the organ says AGREE the independence rule is decoration. (b) Informed impostor: appending a forged agreeing text to an existing source must not raise standing; a forged second SOURCE is the real attack, and its cost is the forging-cost measurement (ledger row 8). (c) A mirror arm and (d) a self-witness arm, each of which must not raise AGREE. (e) A target-swap control: standing computed for another pair must be near the null. (f) Measure independence (k_eff) of the sources actually used: if it is near 1, the AGREE threshold overcounts (row 4). Stamp before scoring; a fresh set read once.

## 6. Open, for the Penelope session and the owner

## 7. A defect found while building this (in Penelope's constraint layer, checked 2026-11-20, not fixed here)
Two contradictory values for one being, in DIFFERENT SENTENCES of ONE document, produce no violation and no gap under `constraint-eoql` when claims carry the grounds `claims-from-rows` produces (`where: "s0"` and `"s1"` with `{ s0: doc, s1: doc }`): sibling grounds never meet. The same two values in one sentence, or with no ground, give a violation. A within-document contradiction is silently missed. The sentence is provenance, not a place; the fix is Penelope's: the document should be the ground those claims hold in (or provenance separated from the `where` axis).
