## B. Queries, gold and strata (reused unchanged)
Queries and gold are exactly score.py's `queries(doc)` (the 25 targets with at least 2 links, by link count) and `anchor_type`. A gold sentence is ZERO by lib.zero_overlap (row 30's definition) and ZERO-CLEAN when it is ZERO and NOT `related(name, sentence)` under the normaliser DECLARED in referent/zero_clean.py (NFKD with marks dropped, lower case, a plural fold on words of 4+ letters, then equal token, shared 5-letter prefix or edit distance 1 on words of 4+ letters). The five functions of zero_clean.py are copied byte for byte into the harness; the sha256 of zero_clean.py as read is recorded in the freeze file. NAMEFREE and DIFFERENT as in score.py. A stratum under 30 gold passages is reported UNJUDGEABLE.

## C. Ranking and scoring
- Each arm ranks every sentence of the document. For the primary comparison EVERY arm (including N0) EXCLUDES the name-bearing sentences, so that no arm is crowded out by sentences that carry the name. A sentence is name-bearing when every token of the name, after fold and plural fold, is among the sentence's tokens. This cannot remove a ZERO or ZERO-CLEAN gold sentence (it has no shared word with the name). N0 is ALSO reported without the exclusion, as row 30 ran it.
- recall@10 is pooled over queries: gold sentences found in the top 10 over gold sentences, per stratum, as in row 30. Precision@10 is pooled hits in the top 10 over sentences returned, over all gold. Intervals are percentile bootstraps over DOCUMENTS (2,000 resamples, seed 20261119). The random-10 null and every shuffle use seed 20261119.
- Ties are broken by document order.

## D. The paraphrase builder and the fold, as built (no regex, no model, no list of words)
- Tokens: Unicode NFKD with combining marks dropped, lower case, a maximal run of alphanumeric characters (str.isalnum, a character loop). The plural fold is zero_clean.py's `stem`.
- N1 query = the first K = 5 name-bearing sentences in document order, joined by a single space, in the document's own wording and order. A query with no name-bearing sentence is NO QUERY: counted as a miss for that arm and reported separately.
- N2 candidate for sentence i = sentences i-1, i, i+1 joined by single spaces in document order (the centre is the one scored; the window is truncated at the document's ends). w = 1 is the isolated sentence (the N0 candidate); w = 5 is reported.
- N3 = N1's query against N2's candidates.
- M1 FOLD: background text = all documents of the set being scored (text only, never links or anchors). Types with at least 5 occurrences have a positive-PMI context vector over a window of +-4 tokens (sparse). For each token w of the name that is a background type, the fold class is {w} union {the words of the same stem} union {v : the robust z of cosine(w, v) against the median and 1.4826 x MAD of cosine(w, u) over all types u is at least sqrt(2 ln V)}, V the number of types. A name token that is not a background type is its own class by stem only. Anchor surprisal = minus the log of the share of the document's sentences carrying any member of the class. A sentence's score = the sum of the surprisals of the distinct anchors it carries, accumulated over the same window w = 3 with each anchor counted once, assigned to the centre sentence.
- U1: the best embedder arm (the one with the highest ZERO-CLEAN recall on DEV) and M1, interleaved round robin, first 10 distinct.
- Controls: TARGET-SWAP builds the same arm from the NEXT query's name in the same document (cyclic) and scores it against the TRUE query's gold; the SHUFFLED-FREQUENT control for F5 is a query of the same token count as N1's, made of the document's most frequent tokens of length at least 4, in seeded random order; random-10.
- Embedder: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 from referent/models, offline, CPU, normalised embeddings, torch 2.15.0, sentence-transformers 6.3.0, max_seq_length 256 (texts longer are truncated by the model's own tokenizer).
