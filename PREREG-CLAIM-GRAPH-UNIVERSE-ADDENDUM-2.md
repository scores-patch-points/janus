# ADDENDUM-2 to PREREG-CLAIM-GRAPH-UNIVERSE (parent 1a37523f89f6567a; ADDENDUM-1 29ce09fac21d8a0d): the relevance gate is the DEF of the VOID. Declared BEFORE any fresh read and before the void-gated arms were scored on anything
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session at its user's correction: "the relevance gate has to do with the DEF of the void". ADDENDUM-1's DEV debugging run showed that when everything connects to everything (hub nouns), stopping on top-10 stability does not bound the universe. A computed statistic (document frequency of a node) would be a second guess at relevance. The Fold already settled what relevance is (eval/void RESULTS: "declaring the void and what would satisfy it is a large, real, null-beating gain; every layer built on hand rules or the engine's name-to-name edges adds nothing"; PREREG L1: the nine-field declared void `declareVoid`, EOVoidDeclaration@1, khora the-fold/void-shape.js, fields filled from the asker's own statement and every unread field a typed gap): a connection is relevant when it bears on the void the asker declared, and the reach ends when no further connection bears on it.

## 1. The void, declared (a DEF with a giver; REFERENT-FIRST rule 10)
For every query class q the void is a plain record with the fields of the khora declaration (not imported: Janus's own code imports no other repo; the fields and their cells are named after it):
- slot (NUL): `identity`, the hole's type: another anchor class of this document that is the same being as q.
- anchor (SIG): q, with the tokens of its mention spans.
- relation (CON): `same-being-as`, defined (below) by the dependency configurations that state or constrain identity.
- admits (INS): UNREAD, a typed gap `kind_unread` (admitting a filler by its induced kind is the Fold's L3, not built here).
- cardinality: `many` (a being may have several aliases). extent: this document.
- giver: the ASKER of DOC-ALIAS (the owner's task statement, 2026-11-20), resting on the Universal Dependencies guidelines as the prior for which configurations state identity. The giver is a named given, not derived from this corpus (the grain theorem).

## 2. What "bears on the void" means (the relation's definition, declared, not tuned)
An edge of the reader's dependency graph bears on `same-being-as` iff it is one of: (a) an apposition (label appos); (b) a copular predicate nominal: a nominal subject (nsubj, any subtype) whose head carries a copula child (label cop), i.e. "X was the Y"; (c) a name edge (flat or flat:name): the parts of one name. Janus's binding candidates (anaphor token to each candidate class, number mismatch excluded, none chosen) are a second admitted connection, in the arm VGB only. Every other edge (objects, obliques, modifiers, conjuncts, compounds) and the shared-lemma merge are NOT in the relation: they connect two things in one scene, never one thing to itself, and the shared-lemma merge is only the same word. The ONLY merge is the anchor handle (all tokens of a class's mention spans are one node). No other list exists in the arm: the gate reads edges only through the void's relation definition (a guard test checks this).
Disclosure: ADDENDUM-1's DEV debugging run was seen before this was written (the lemma merge hurt; the unconditional graph gave 0.124 against the bare label's 0.483). The lemma merge is excluded here because the void's relation does not contain it, and the relation was written from the asker's task and the UD guidelines, not fitted to DEV.

## 3. Reach (relevance, not a constant)
The universe of q = the own sentences of q plus the sentences of every node reachable from q by traversing ONLY edges that bear on the void, to exhaustion: the reach ends when the ring has no further edge that bears on the void. There is no TAU in the gated arms. HMAXF = 8 stays a safety cap (a run still growing at 8 is counted unstable).

## 4. Arms, controls, falsifiers (ctxvoid.py)
- VG: the void-gated universe (without bindings). VGB: with binding edges. VGR: the VGB graph's structurally REACHED classes listed first (by hop, then by order in the document), then the VGB ranking fills to ten (the reach as a nomination, not only a context).
- Controls: A0 (bare anchor); NG (the ungated graph, anchor merge only, ADDENDUM-1's FG_NOLEM with its top-10 rule); VG_RAND (the strings-only TWIN of the gate: the same NUMBER of dependency edges as the void's relation admits in this document, chosen at random, seeded, grown the same way); T_VG (hop-0 sentences plus random sentences to the size VGB reached); ADJF (adjacency, ADDENDUM-1's rule); PROX; target-swap.
- Falsifiers: H1 VGB does not beat A0; H2 does not beat T_VG; H3 does not beat ADJF; H4 does not beat VG_RAND (the DEF of relevance adds nothing over a random edge set of the same size); H5 does not beat NG (gating adds nothing over no gate); H6 swap ratio >= 0.5; H7 VGB does not beat VG (bindings add nothing); H8 precision@10 under half of A0; H9 VGR does not beat VGB (the reached classes add nothing as a nomination). Thresholds as the parent (document-bootstrap interval above 0 and a difference of at least 0.05 recall@10). Strata DISJOINT, and GROWN_DISJOINT (the universe grew beyond q's own sentences) judged where it holds at least 30 gold; where the gate leaves most universes at their own sentences the twin equals the arm there and H2 and H4 cannot speak: that is reported as a finding about how often identity is stated in the document, never as a pass.
- A pass needs H2 to H5 NOT hit. A hit names the part that was strings.

## 5. What this does not test
The void's `admits` (kind induction) and its use to admit fillers (the Fold's L3); the void defined by Penelope as a DEF in the log (this is the harness's declaration, the asker's, with the giver named); other languages; whether a model fold engine reports more. A nomination result is not an identity: sameness is declared by a giver.
