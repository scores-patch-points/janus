## DATING CAVEAT, from the owner (2026-11-20): all of this predates the intensive work on each capacity of the cube
Every experiment below is from 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-15, before the cube-era work: Penelope's DEF, EVA and REC as separate declared, tested and revised acts; the phasepost writer audit; Janus as a structure-only researcher; the identity prior and declaration; the binding evidence; the referent-first rules. The Fold's per-operator checks (ants F1-F3) were pre-registered by their own author as regex and word-list implementations ("DEF_RX accepts any copula", "CMP is a word list"). So treat every number below as a BASELINE FOR THE OLD STACK (a 2B model, small n, pre-cube checks), never as a ceiling for the current one. What carries over is: the measurement DESIGNS (frozen batteries with gold validated from page bytes, positive and negative controls, nulls, replay of recorded fetches, the mouth rule that a repair may never add a content word), the negative results that do not depend on the architecture (a model grading its own output reads nothing; a line-level fold equals its null; a small model commits and is wrong more), and the failure patterns (template skills fall to paraphrase). The numbers themselves would have to be re-measured on the cube-era stack.

## What this changes (my reading, not the Fold's)
1. "How far can the LLM mouth be pushed" HAD a measured answer for the October stack (the P1 gate plus the Pivot, with a 2B model and pre-cube checks), and the draft-lint design (a pre-registration with falsifiers, no results) is the mouth-fidelity test already written: its rules, that the linter may never add a content word and that a win scored by the generator is void, should be reused. 2. The template-skill paraphrase failure (1 of 14) is the string-versus-referent circle in the Fold's own data.
3. The void's L2 and L3 (kind induction over the referent's universe) are the open pieces that match what our binding evidence lacks. The void work is the Fold's earlier version of the universe idea.
