# PREREG: can Wilson's ants generate novel programs that reduce surprise while following the dependency order? (frozen before the harness is run; nothing scored)
Written 2026-11-20 by the Janus session at its user's direction ("research our ability to use wilson's ants to generate novel programs to reduce surprise while following the dependency order", then "make it so"). Background read, not run: khora kernel/stigmergy.js (trails: deposits, evaporation, learned order, scouts), organs/structure-swarm.js (ants build pipelines step by step following trails; a pipeline is reported only if it beats a SEARCH-AWARE ceiling: the best score any tried pipeline reaches on a null copy), eval/lavar/eo-swarm.mjs and capacity-swarm.mjs (breed CON·Figure, differentiate SEG·Figure, an admission gate that rises as the search widens). Stamp: sha256 first 16 hex in PREREG-ANT-COLONY-SURPRISE.md.sha256short.

## 1. The question, and what a "program" is here
A PROGRAM is an ordered sequence of up to DEPTH moves, each a transformation of the token stream; its SURPRISE REDUCTION is the bits it saves on the stream as encountered (segmentation-surprise.mjs: an adaptive Dirichlet-process unigram over word types; alpha = 1, an ARBITRARY CONSTANT of ours, labelled). The moves are DATA-DERIVED, not a fixed list: every word-final string of 1 to 4 characters that ends at least MIN = 20 distinct word types and fires at least 20 times on the learning stream becomes a candidate (MIN = 20 is an ARBITRARY CONSTANT of ours, labelled). Two kinds: `cut:s` (operator SEG) splits s off the end of every current token that ends with s, leaving a stem; `join:s` (operator CON) re-attaches a standalone piece that an earlier `cut:s` produced to its left neighbour. The DEPENDENCY ORDER is the helix: a move at operator k may read only what moves at operators up to k produced. So WITH THE GUARD ON: all cuts (SEG) come before any join (CON), and `join:s` is allowed only after `cut:s` in the same program. WITH THE GUARD OFF: any move in any order. The ants' only memory is trails (khora's own stigmergy functions routeOrderFor and deposit, imported by the harness, never edited): a step is chosen by the learned order for the head `prog|after:<previous move>`, with scouts at rate EXPLORE = 0.35 (borrowed from structure-swarm.js, labelled an unmeasured constant); a program stops at DEPTH = 4 or with probability STOP = 0.25 after a step (labelled). A program with positive gain lays a trail on each of its steps; failures deposit nothing.

## 2. Material (rule 11, stated)
The learning stream is the raw sentence text of UD_English-EWT train (CC BY-SA; the sentences the treebank carries as "# text = "), tokenized by khora's generic tokenizer (no prior). Held-out A: EWT dev; held-out B: GUM dev (local, non-commercial licence: only numbers are reported, nothing is written to a log). The TEST files are not touched. This experiment is at the SURFACE level of REFERENT-FIRST rule 11 and declared so in eot_law.py: it decides what counts as a token, upstream of the codec; the gold tokenization is read ONLY by the gold-agreement report, never by an ant or by the score.

## 3. Arms (the same evaluation budget each: ROUNDS = 8 generations of ANTS = 24 ants, 192 programs per run; 5 seeds, 20261119 + 0 to 4)
- ANT: trails and scouts, guard on. ANT_NOGUARD: trails and scouts, guard off. RAND: the strings-only twin (rule 3): programs drawn uniformly from the allowed moves, no trails, guard on, same budget.
- KNOWN: the reference program of the six clitic rules in force on the log's verdicts (`'s n't 'm 'll 've 're` as cuts), scored the same way, not searched.
- THE SEARCH-AWARE CEILING (as structure-swarm.js): for each run, every tried program is also applied to a CHARACTER-PERMUTED copy of the stream (each token's characters permuted, seeded: word-internal structure destroyed, token inventory shape kept); the ceiling is the largest gain any tried program reaches there. A program is FOUND only if its real gain exceeds that ceiling. A wider search raises the bar.

## 4. Measures (per run, then medians over the 5 seeds)
Best gain found (bits); the number of programs that are FOUND; evaluations until a program within 90% of the run's best is first tried; WASTED evaluations (programs whose gain is not positive, or that leave the stream unchanged); for the best program: prospective gain on held-out A and B (the model built on the learning stream prices text it never saw), and GOLD AGREEMENT: token-boundary F1 on EWT dev against the treebank's gold tokenization, beside the baseline tokenizer's (0.9820, measured today). Reported, not gated: the moves the best programs contain.

## 5. Falsifiers, written first
- A1: the median best gain of ANT does not exceed that of RAND (trails add nothing over random search at equal budget).
- A2: ANT's WASTED fraction is not below ANT_NOGUARD's and its median best gain is not above ANT_NOGUARD's (the dependency-order guard adds nothing).
- A3: for at least half of the ANT runs the best program's prospective gain is not positive on BOTH held-out corpora (it fits the stream and does not generalize).
- A4: in at least half of the ANT runs no program is FOUND (nothing beats what the character-permuted copy gives: no structure beyond the search itself).
- A5 (reported as an eye, not a verdict on the colony): the best ANT program's gold boundary F1 is below the baseline's (0.9820): compression-optimal slicing disagrees with the treebank's word units. Expected, since compression finds morphemes ("-s", "-ing") where UD cuts words and clitics; a hit here means the criteria are different eyes that must both look, not that the colony failed.
- The claim that Wilson's ants can generate novel programs that reduce surprise under the dependency order is supported only if A1 to A4 are NOT hit. A5 is the finding about what "meaning unit" each eye sees.

## 6. Limits
English only; one parser-free surface stream; the moves are suffix cuts and joins (a small program language: no merges across words, no reorderings); the colony is a re-implementation of structure-swarm's walk with khora's stigmergy functions imported, not khora's own `colony()` (its move lists and score fields are fixed constants of the file, which this experiment does not edit); the score is a unigram model (a bigram model would price closed-class clitics differently: a follow-up). DEV discipline: nothing is tuned after the run; any change is an addendum stamped before a fresh read; the numbers are DEV (EWT dev and GUM dev are seen here for the first time as held-out measures of programs, not as tuning data).
